## What the workflow now records

- **Treatment context and history.** Staff explicitly choose maintenance, initiation, Day 1, Day 8, restart, or switching, and can record indication, prior product/dose, history source, and a shared treatment-plan reference. A recorded prior administration date requires its source. Days, weeks, and calendar months remain distinct; month calculations clamp to month-end. The displayed interval comparison helps review the order and does not choose a return date or clear treatment.
- **Explicit review.** General and product-specific screening answers start unanswered. Concerns and not-applicable answers require details. Nonroutine timing, a non-maintenance phase, or a screening concern requires a documented provider decision to proceed, with provider, contact time, instructions, and reference. Hold/clarify decisions block review. The server validates the expected checks and stores canonical wording and the guidance version; viewing a reference does not attest that a check was performed.
- **Product references.** The versioned guide covers 13 distinct products: Aristada Initio, Aristada, Invega Sustenna, Invega Trinza, Invega Hafyera, Erzofri, Abilify Maintena, Abilify Asimtufii, Uzedy, Vivitrol, haloperidol decanoate, fluphenazine decanoate, and Zyprexa Relprevv. It separates review prompts, preparation, needle considerations, incomplete-delivery guidance, and counseling. Zyprexa Relprevv is blocked from the normal review pathway because its specialist setting is not implemented. Unknown or ambiguous names receive general checks and a prompt to verify the current label and order. See [the source and version audit](injection-guidance-sources.md).
- **Actual outcome and follow-up.** Administration distinguishes complete, partial, not delivered, and dose/route/site error outcomes, with actual dose, site, and route. An explicit “no route or site used” attestation is available only for not delivered; other outcomes require the actual route and site. A route differing from the order requires the error pathway and follow-up narrative. Recording an administration or attempt consumes its reserved whole packages once; unopened, unused supply should be released through Hold instead. Staff can record counseling actually provided, follow-up instructions, and observation duration/outcome; declined, unnecessary, or transferred observation requires an explanation. These fields record facts rather than supplying normal findings by default.
- **Documents and encounter history.** Formatted note and English/Spanish AVS previews support plain-text copying and printing, preserve finalized snapshots and attributed amendments, and distinguish reviewed, administered, held, cancelled, incomplete, and error outcomes. Free-text instructions retain their original language, with a warning to review untranslated text in the Spanish AVS. Tebra filing remains a separate recorded action. The patient timeline shows up to eight other encounters from the currently loaded records; staff must check Tebra for older or missing history.

## Compatibility and limits

The upgrade adds fields to the existing encounter snapshots and uses the current transactional encounter/inventory storage. It requires no SQL migration. Historical records remain readable; a legacy reviewed encounter without the current assessment and guidance version must be reviewed again before administration. Editing an order invalidates its review, and cleared optional history/context fields no longer retain stale values.

Each physical injection remains an independent encounter and stock transaction. Encounters for the same patient are grouped only when their treatment-plan references match exactly. A linked plan does not establish that another injection or an oral component was administered; there is no unified oral-medication administration ledger.

The app does not select doses, authorize restart regimens, calculate clinical eligibility, or replace provider direction. Product references do not verify the physical stock or establish clinical clearance. The error outcome improves truthful documentation within the existing prospective workflow; it still requires prior review and the same-day checks, so a dedicated retrospective or back-entry pathway is not implemented.

This upgrade does not authorize new PHI hosting or real-patient use. The evaluation's existing access, hosting, recovery, and clinical-readiness limits continue to apply. Live workflow acceptance and staff review of the new output remain necessary before claiming this upgrade is ready.
