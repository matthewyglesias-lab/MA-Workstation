# Injection workflow review

Reviewed 2026-09-15 against the existing repository and current manufacturer/NIH DailyMed prescribing information. This is an engineering review of a clinic helper, not a prescribing protocol, clinical approval, or evidence of a deployed service. The active Tebra order and verified chart remain authoritative.

## First usable scope

Build one complete injection encounter around an identified patient, with inventory and Tebra handoff attached. Keep other service types out of the primary navigation for this release. Routine maintenance can use the shortest path; initiation, restart, switching, uncertain history, incomplete delivery, and exceptions need a documented provider plan. The app must never choose a dose or restart regimen.

1. **Patient and order.** Verify two identifiers against Tebra for this encounter. Record chart ID, order reference/date, ordering provider, exact brand/formulation, ordered dose and units, route, schedule, and ordered due date. Capture the relevant order as a staff-verified snapshot with actor and time; do not imply automatic chart synchronization. Record the actual prior administration date, product/dose, source, and whether history is unknown. An appointment date is not a prior administration date.
2. **Review.** Capture current allergy review, changes/concerns, patient agreement or decline, and any applicable product checks. Do not precheck clinical attestations, normal findings, consent, or tolerance. Clearly distinguish unanswered, confirmed, and needs review. When timing or history needs review, record the provider, decision, time, and reference; invalidate that review after its patient/product/dose/date facts change.
3. **Supply and preparation.** Choose the physical lot that matches the ordered formulation and strength. Confirm NDC, expiration, ownership, storage/quarantine status, available supply, actual site, and product-specific preparation. Patient-owned supply must only serve that patient. Expired/quarantined stock must not be selected as ready. A reservation means supply is held, not administered.
4. **Record outcome.** Require an explicit outcome: given, held, declined, or escalated. Capture actual date/time, administered dose, route/site, administering staff, response, and any exception. Distinguish a partial or failed delivery from a full dose. No completion control should imply that a dose was physically administered. For a medication error that already happened, preserve truthful exception documentation and escalation instead of forcing invented safe values.
5. **Finish.** Generate a factual note and AVS, commit the encounter and related stock disposition once, and track Tebra filing separately. Show who filed it, when, and the reference. Printing/copying is not proof of filing. Corrections create an attributed amendment; they must not silently rewrite a completed record.

The current stock ledger uses whole inventory units. For single-use kits, one consumed kit and the clinical administered amount are separate facts. Do not imply that it supports fractional vial balances, multi-dose vial beyond-use dating, partial replacement dosing, or controlled wastage accounting until those features are implemented and tested.

## Reuse assessment

Strong reusable structures exist in `src/domain/injection.ts`: the typed encounter, phase/disposition separation, paired-component traceability, explicit provider-plan fields, and fingerprints invalidating stale timing review. `injection-needle.ts` has test cases for the 90-kg paliperidone threshold, body habitus, supplied needles, and unresolved inputs. `injection-avs-content.ts` and `injection-avs-render.ts` already distinguish held from administered visits. Their tests cover malformed dates, bilingual content, paired injections, hostile input, and printing. These are useful implementation patterns and regression cases, not proof of current clinical correctness.

Resolve these concrete issues before using legacy outputs as clinical decision support:

- `effectiveInjectionCadence` and the reference bundle deliberately use 56/84/182 days for some two/three/six-month regimens. A comment in `evaluateTimingWithCadence` says calendar-month arithmetic is used, but the implementation calls the fixed-day calculation. The tests explicitly preserve same-weekday behavior. Do not transfer this approximation as label-derived timing. Store the verified ordered date; represent weeks and calendar months distinctly if calculation is later added.
- `effectiveInjectionWindow` supplies generic windows for unsupported interval selections. `orderVerify` neutral statuses do not mean an injection is clinically cleared. A generic late threshold based on 1.5 times the interval cannot replace product-specific missed-dose review.
- The Uzedy catalog supports schizophrenia strength/interval combinations without an indication dimension. It also describes the needle as attached while the source instructions describe attaching the separately supplied needle. Correct these before showing them as current administration instructions.
- Catalog `defaultSite`, technique suggestions, normal responses, and expected safety checks must remain suggestions. They are not observations and must not be persisted as performed without explicit staff confirmation.
- `InjectionEncounter` is a good clinical snapshot, but the new console needs server-side validation and transactional encounter/stock linkage; a browser engine alone cannot protect against a second staff member, duplicate retry, stale version, or patient switch.
- Existing patient-screening content declares clinic approval in its metadata. That historical string is not approval of the new application, new wording, or migrated workflow.

## Documentation and AVS

The note should be concise, chronological, and fact-only: verified order/history; current screening and unresolved concerns; actual product/dose/route/site/date/time and lot/NDC/expiration; staff attribution; response; exception or provider communication; follow-up plan. Never infer normal vitals, no allergies, tolerability, completed observation, education, or an oral dose from a default or merely verified plan. Include independently administered components only when recorded as given.

The AVS should show what actually happened, the provider-ordered next step/date, and relevant aftercare. A held/declined encounter must not say the patient received an injection. Do not display a generic administration window as permission to attend or receive medication. Keep patient instructions simple; retain the clinic's preferred walk-in/call-ahead instructions only after confirming those operational settings. Medication-specific counseling remains linked to the actual product and current source.

## Tomorrow readiness

At review time, repository documentation states that the new App Service/Azure SQL resources have not been provisioned and that the existing Static Web App preview has hit its staging-environment limit. The standalone preview is fictional, browser-local and resettable. These are separate from clinical workflow completion. Passing SQL Server integration tests does not establish a deployed Azure SQL connection, production access controls, durable storage, or recovery.

A credible next-day deliverable is a tested injection-first build and synthetic staff rehearsal. Real-patient use requires evidence that the target backend/database is deployed, migrations and staff identity work, inventory and encounter saves survive reload/restart and duplicate retries, access is enforced server-side, and Tebra reconciliation/print output have been exercised by staff. PIN entry must still resolve a named staff identity with server-verified credentials, throttling, expiration and audit attribution; a shared or browser-only PIN gate cannot establish that identity. Keep the existing clinical workflow available until those actual deployment checks are complete.

Minimum meaningful scenarios: routine maintenance; unknown prior dose; altered patient after review; expired/wrong-owner stock; concurrent last-kit reservation; duplicate submit after lost response; mismatched dose/lot; stale encounter edit; held/declined with no stock use; incomplete delivery with explicit inventory disposition; provider-directed restart; paired component missing or expired; truthful error record; note/AVS with no invented findings; logout/idle lock and rejected unauthenticated API access. Do not claim all scenarios passed merely because they are listed here.
